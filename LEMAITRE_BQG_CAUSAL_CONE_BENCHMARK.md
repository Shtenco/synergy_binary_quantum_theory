## 1. Purpose and scientific boundary

This benchmark imports the result of Philip A. LeMaitre and T. Rick Perche, *Spacetime Emergence in the Memory of a Learning Agent* (arXiv:2611.03596, v1, 2026-11-02), as an **independent operational reconstruction control**.

It does not treat that paper as evidence that spacetime is generated by binary degrees of freedom, nor as a derivation of BQG, photons, Maxwell theory, or general relativity.

The paper's environment already contains a real massless scalar quantum field on a prescribed 3+1-dimensional Minkowski background. The experiment holds two spatial directions fixed and studies a 1+1 slice. The agent learns a task on the resulting quantum-mutual-information landscape. Therefore it tests whether an agent can recover causal structure encoded in quantum data, not whether the quantum data or Minkowski geometry emerge from binary microdynamics.

The BQG side must remain independently sourced from BQG microscopic dynamics. No paper metric, light-cone mask, target slope, or Minkowski reward surface may be fed into the BQG generator.

## 3. Source-level audit findings that must remain visible

The notebook is not literally coordinate-free:

- The environment stores integer grid indices internally but passes physical `(x,t)` coordinates to the encoder.
- The coordinate axes are explicitly supplied through `Iparams=[x_axis,t_axis,T,sigma]`; the axes span `[-10,10]` with a `301 x 301` grid.
- The action alphabet contains the eight nearest-neighbour displacements in the two coordinate directions, including diagonals.
- A decoder is enabled and trained to reconstruct the coordinate input; the manifest records a 20-dimensional latent state and nonzero decoder reconstruction losses.
- The objective is the mutual information of two detector systems, but the training signal is not just an unmodified scalar QMI: the notebook applies a monotone normalized-power transform with exponent 0.25 and adds dense-objective and progress/downhill reward terms.
- The metric is not read directly from the trained network. An external post-processing stage forms a scalar field from the softmax average of planner action scores, applies a local-neighbour prominence mask, extracts ridge branches, fixes `g_xx=1`, and fits a quadratic form.
- The reported tensor is therefore a reconstructed 1+1 Lorentzian representative after a conformal normalization, not a complete metric reconstruction.

The authors themselves identify additional needed checks: coordinate transformations, different detector smearings, independent signalling-sensitive observables, and extension beyond a flat 1+1 slice. Quantum mutual information contains spacelike vacuum correlations and is not a pure signalling measure.

## 4. BQG reference already available

The BQG repository contains a separate exact result for a **reduced free-fermion model**:

```text
E_±(k) = ±sqrt(4 m^2 + 9 cos^2(k))
v_front(m) = sqrt(4 m^2 + 9) - 2 |m|
v_front(0) = 3
```

Its dimensionless massless front is `v_front(0)/3 = 1`. The normalization by 3 is a comparison of units, not a new physical calibration. The result is not the physical speed of light of the full BQG theory. The existing BQG documentation correctly leaves the physical Lorentzian limit, physical relational generator, common matter/gravity cone, and full-refinement stability open.

The offline gate compares only the two dimensionless null slopes and verifies the analytic reduced-model identities. It must never promote the finite reference comparison to a physical BQG closure.

## 5. Required experiment sequence

### Stage A — exact external reproduction

1. Pin the exact paper version, notebook blob SHA, manifest SHA, metric artifact SHA, and all five evaluation JSON SHAs.
2. Recompute the raw detector QMI landscape independently from the notebook's analytic Mathematica-derived expressions.
3. Compare NumPy evaluation with high-precision SymPy evaluation at randomly selected points, near the small-separation branch, and on both sides of the candidate null ridges.
4. Recompute the normalized-power objective and all reward components independently.
5. Load the saved checkpoints and reproduce the five-agent planner diagnostics.
6. Reproduce the 505-point branch extraction and the unweighted quadratic fit exactly.
7. Report numerical error, not only a PASS label.

A failure to reproduce any stage blocks all downstream comparisons.

### Stage B — blind reconstruction controls on the external task

Run preregistered ablations with new seeds:

- Hide physical coordinate values from the agent; expose only opaque state IDs and action outcomes. Do not provide a decoder target in physical coordinates.
- Randomly permute state IDs independently of physical position.
- Apply affine and nonlinear monotone coordinate reparameterizations; transform only the final inferred tensor by the independently known Jacobian.
- Remove dense-objective, progress, and downhill shaping; train on raw QMI-derived reward only.
- Compare raw QMI, transformed QMI, commutator/retarded response, and Hadamard/symmetric-correlation objectives separately.
- Remove the edge-start curriculum and test uniform starts.
- Compare planner-score, reward-model, and policy-only reconstructions.
- Fit the cone on one subset of spacetime points and evaluate prediction error on held-out regions.
- Use negative controls: Euclidean diffusion, a nonrelativistic kernel, shuffled reward landscape, and a kernel with a different propagation speed.

The key question is whether the inferred null structure survives removal of coordinate and post-processing shortcuts.

### Stage C — BQG operational reconstruction

Do not transplant the Minkowski reward landscape into BQG.

1. Start from a BQG microscopic binary state and the actual theory-specific transition/constraint operators.
2. Derive a physical or explicitly labelled finite-history amplitude. Do not treat the master-constraint heat-kernel parameter as physical time.
3. Construct local operational probes and compute their response/correlation from the BQG amplitude. Keep commutator/retarded response distinct from symmetric vacuum correlations.
4. Give the learner only probe outcomes, local intervention labels, and opaque relational state identifiers. Do not provide an embedding, graph-distance matrix, metric, light-cone mask, or target speed.
5. Let the learner infer an event relation and candidate causal order from interventions and responses.
6. Reconstruct a coarse Lorentzian cone from the inferred relation, using held-out events and multiple independent estimators.
7. Repeat across binary graph generations, q sectors, refinement maps, states, and local perturbations.
8. Test whether the same cone is obtained for matter and gravitational perturbations.

The current route-graph smoothing model has an explicit factor `2^k` in `n_micro=(2B)^k 2^k`; this inserts one scaling direction into the smoothing ansatz. It is not, by itself, a derivation of time or a dynamical causal cone. The reduced free-fermion front is a separate finite model and must be labelled as such.

## 6. Hard acceptance criteria for an emergence claim

A BQG causal-emergence claim is blocked until all of the following pass:

1. **No coordinate leakage:** the learner never receives physical coordinates, embedding coordinates, metric coefficients, or target cone labels.
2. **No target-shaped reward:** the reward is computed from BQG operational dynamics, not from an inserted Minkowski/QMI target surface.
3. **Intervention causality:** causal order is inferred from response/commutator support or controlled interventions, not mutual information alone.
4. **Blind held-out prediction:** inferred causal relations predict unseen intervention outcomes.
5. **Refinement stability:** the cone converges under graph refinement and is not an artefact of one finite graph.
6. **Coordinate covariance:** equivalent relabellings and admissible coordinate changes yield the same geometric content.
7. **Universality:** independent probes and microscopic initial states recover the same IR cone.
8. **Matter/gravity agreement:** both sectors share the same limiting cone.
9. **Lorentzian signature:** the reconstructed quadratic form has one negative and the remaining positive eigenvalues in the IR.
10. **Physical-time provenance:** the evolution parameter is relationally derived or otherwise justified as physical; it is not silently identified with a computational or constraint parameter.

Passing the LeMaitre reference gate is necessary for validating the comparison machinery, but is neither necessary nor sufficient for BQG geometrogenesis. The physical Maxwell and physical TT gates remain separate and open until derived from the same theory-specific physical generating functional.
