## 1. Causal notions that must not be conflated

Keep three layers separate:

- **Constraint-support order:** which graph sectors can be connected by products of the regulated constraints. This is an algebraic support statement.
- **Relational history order:** ordering defined by a clock or by physical boundary/history data after constraint reduction. This is physical only after the clock/history observable and inner product are derived.
- **Lorentzian propagation:** support or a controlled tail of physical retarded response between relationally localized observables. This is the layer from which a spacetime causal cone may be reconstructed.

A symmetric positive master operator can constrain graph-sector distance through its powers. Its heat-kernel parameter is not a clock. The zero-mode projector alone loses nonzero-spectrum support information and cannot recover a unique causal order.

## 2. Candidate internal clock: volume is a hypothesis, not an input

The first candidate to test is the theory's own geometric volume observable (V), because it is already present in the graph-changing operator construction. It is **not** declared monotone or physical time.

At a fixed regulator, for the actual ordered constraint (C_\epsilon), form the clock-flow diagnostic

[
D_V^{(\epsilon)}=\frac{i}{\hbar}[C_\epsilon,V_\epsilon].
]

This is only a diagnostic of whether volume can serve as a local clock in a specified sector. It does not turn the gauge parameter of (C_\epsilon) into time.

A volume-clock sector is admissible only if all of the following are demonstrated on the same physical carrier:

1. (V_\epsilon) is a well-defined self-adjoint observable on the declared common domain.
2. After constraint and gauge reduction, the relevant matrix elements of (D_V^{(\epsilon)}) have a nonzero, stable sign on the proposed clock sector. Report the full spectrum/distribution and state dependence; do not report only its mean.
3. Turning points, recurrences, zero-velocity subspaces and branch changes are identified. If they occur, restrict the claim to a stated local patch or reject volume as a global clock.
4. A factorization of the actual constraint is derived, not assumed:
   [
   C_\epsilon \simeq A_\epsilon(V)\big(P_V+H_{\rm rel}(V)\big),
   ]
   or an explicitly derived alternative. The branch, operator ordering, domain, self-adjointness and inverse of (A_\epsilon) must be controlled.
5. Conditional evolution generated by (H_{\rm rel}) reproduces the original constrained matrix elements and preserves all constraints. The residual must be measured on the physical domain and under refinement.
6. The induced physical inner product is positive and non-degenerate after quotienting null states.

A finite-dimensional truncation cannot satisfy the exact canonical commutator ([V,P_V]=i\hbar I), since the trace of a finite matrix commutator is zero. Therefore a finite calculation may test an interior wave-packet approximation and boundary errors, but must not claim an exact finite-dimensional canonical pair. The approximation must improve with cutoff/refinement and be reported with its boundary leakage.

If the volume diagnostic changes sign or has no stable sector, do not tune a different clock to fit the desired cone. Either derive another genuine degree of freedom from the BQG microscopic carrier, or use the boundary-history route below without inventing a time coordinate.

## 5. Physical retarded response: definition and non-substitution rules

Only after a physical clock or boundary-history ordering, physical inner product, physical state and gauge-invariant relational observables have been derived may the theory define

[
G^R_{AB}(\tau)=
-\frac{i}{\hbar}\,\theta(\tau)
\langle[O_A(\tau),O_B(0)]\rangle_{\rm phys}.
]

The same response must be checked by a controlled source intervention: perturb the source coupled to (O_B), compare the physical expectation of (O_A) with/without the intervention, and verify agreement with the linear-response limit.

Do not substitute any of the following:

- kinematical commutators or symmetric correlators;
- mutual information or a QMI ridge;
- master-constraint heat-kernel flow;
- a constraint resolvent spectral parameter (z);
- an externally supplied clock or background metric;
- per-slice normalization that forces a stable-looking front.

Physical distance must be reconstructed from relational metric observables on the same physical history/carrier. The spectral metric must be obtained independently from the physical effective action, not calibrated using the causal front it is meant to check.
