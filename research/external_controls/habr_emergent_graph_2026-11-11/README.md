## Independence contract

1. This audit is a separate research branch of BQG. It does not modify or redefine the canonical BQG model.
2. No formula, coefficient, exponent, normalization, particle assignment, or numerical result from the target article is accepted without an independent derivation.
3. Every displayed mathematical expression is entered in the formula ledger with source location, assumptions, dimensions, derivation status, independent implementation, test, and verdict.
4. Distinguish exact identities, asymptotic statements, numerical fits, definitions, assumptions, and physical claims. Never label a fit as a derivation.
5. Preserve the target's original values and code; all corrections are recorded as separate findings.
6. All fits are quarantined from prediction tests. Held-out data may not be used to refit the global scale or formula exponents.
7. Correlated observables and quantities defined from one another count as one dependency cluster, not independent confirmations.
8. Report failures and inconclusive results as first-class outcomes. No PASS from a plotting script or self-reported summary.

## Initial findings (provisional, based on direct source inspection)

- The article's geometric resonance is a definition/criticality ansatz unless an independent dynamical derivation is supplied.
- The article's displayed 3D lattice sum \(\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z^3\setminus\{0\}}|n|^{-2}\) diverges; a finite value requires a specified cutoff/regularization and cannot equal \(K\zeta(2)\) as written.
- For a finite connected graph, \(\zeta_G(0)=N-1\) under the usual nonzero-spectrum definition by direct counting; this is distinct from the claimed \(N(2K)^{3/2}\) density-of-states expression.
- The local edge current and continuum metric claims require additional hypotheses and derivations; they do not follow from the displayed definitions alone.
- The public stress-test implementation contains a global fit of \(N\) to the same constants subsequently discussed as predictions; its LOO procedure refits \(N\) on the remaining constants.
- The public variation script inserts \(\mu_c=2/3\) and free-energy coefficients as inputs; its minimization does not independently derive that exponent.

These findings are initial and do not substitute for the complete formula-by-formula audit.
