# W5 SU(2) structural basis contract

## Purpose

`scripts/bqg_w5_su2_structural_basis.py` enumerates admissible representation
labels for a fixed ten-link K5 spin assignment. Its support is defined only by
SU(2) tensor-product rules; no amplitude cutoff, numerical coefficient, CG
overlap, or eigensolver output is consulted.

## Conventions

All spins are doubled integers. For two representations,

`s1 tensor s2 = |s1-s2|, |s1-s2|+2, ..., s1+s2`.

At a four-valent node, pair channels are
`K12 in s1 tensor s2` and `K34 in s3 tensor s4`.

* Gauss-singlet labels obey `K12=K34=K`.
* An explicit charged source label obeys
  `|K12-K34| <= J2 <= K12+K34` and
  `K12+K34+J2 = 0 mod 2`, with all `M2=-J2,-J2+2,...,J2`.

The covariant K5 key is `(spins, labels)`: four nodes carry `("G",K)`;
the selected source carries `("C",J2,M2,K12,K34)`. This matches the label
conventions used by the existing covariant K-leg implementation.

## W5 seed

The five frozen seeds have ten doubled link spins equal to 4 and node labels
`K=(0,2,4,6,8)`. Their source-covariant embedding uses `J2=M2=0` and
`K12=K34=K` at the source node.

## Scope and non-claims

This is an exact representation-theoretic *ambient basis* for each supplied
spin sector. It is not the operator-reachable support of the full 24-term
epsilon KKV map. The operator may occupy a strict subspace because of
intertwiners, orientation, holonomy-index contractions, volume blocks and
coherent cancellations. Conversely, this generator does not decide whether
a coefficient is nonzero.

The full W5 reachable-basis problem still requires a word-aware support
propagator through the actual forward and independently reversed-adjoint
operator words, with every intermediate link-spin sector represented. No
physical projector, HDA closure, or nonzero physical kernel is implied.


## Exact dimension of the initial W5 spin sector

For the frozen seed spin assignment `spins=(4,)*10` (ten links with
physical spin j=2), every node has local quartet `(4,4,4,4)`.

* Each ordinary Gauss node has five singlet channels: `K=0,2,4,6,8`.
* The complete charged source factor has 625 labels when all allowed `J2`,
  magnetic `M2`, and `(K12,K34)` channels are included.
* With one charged source and four Gauss nodes, the full factorized ambient
  covariant basis therefore has
  `625 * 5^4 = 390625` label keys.

This is a finite, exact combinatorial count for one fixed link-spin sector.
It is **not** the size of the operator-reachable W5 subspace and does not
include other spin sectors generated by holonomy hits.

## Implementation threshold warning

The existing operator path still contains coefficient-based filtering below
the final matrix accumulator. In particular, the covariant projection and
charged-node decomposition routines use small absolute cutoffs when retaining
recoupling overlaps, and the covariant completion conversion has a default
tolerance. Therefore using the fixed-basis matrix assembler at the final stage
does not by itself make the current W5 computation threshold-free. A genuinely
threshold-free coefficient pipeline requires exact structural projection
support plus unpruned coefficient propagation at every intermediate stage.


## Word-aware link-spin support

The generator now also contains a conservative propagator for the repository's
Peter-Weyl word format. A `P` path applies one fundamental hit to each
successive edge; in doubled-spin labels each hit allows only
`s -> s-1` or `s -> s+1`, subject to the declared link cutoff. A `V`
operation leaves link spins unchanged. The adapter
`t_sequence_spin_support` imports the actual `T_sequences` and
`adjoint_sequence` definitions from the existing Peter-Weyl engine, avoiding
a duplicated operator-word template. In the current call graph these words
are the `H_E` primitive templates consumed by the K-block construction; they
are not the complete outer `C(K)C(K)C(V)` word of the W5 Lorentzian operator.

For a representative frozen `H_E` primitive with `(v,a,b,c)=(0,1,2,3)`, initial
`spins=(4,)*10`, and `Jmax2=10`, the edge-hit multiset is:
`(0,3)` twice and `(0,1),(1,2),(0,2)` once each. The edge-spin-only
superset therefore contains `3 * 2^3 = 24` link-spin assignments before
local intertwiner constraints. This count is a combinatorial consequence of
the word's hit pattern, not a computed KKV amplitude.

This propagator still does not impose magnetic-index CG non-vanishing,
four-valent tensor overlap support, volume-block matrix zeros, or coherent
epsilon cancellation. It is deliberately an upper bound. The next layer must
propagate admissible node labels over these spin sectors and then compare the
resulting conservative support with actual unthresholded coefficient columns.


## Ambient-basis cost for one H_E primitive word family

For the 24 spin assignments in the representative `T_sequences` family above,
the complete one-source covariant ambient basis sizes (four Gauss nodes and
one complete charged source) range from **57,600** to **630,000** keys.
The sum of the 24 per-sector dimensions is **5,759,800**.

These are exact counts from the triangle/parity label enumeration. They show
why a full Cartesian materialization is wasteful: the operator-reachable
subspace should be stored sparsely, but its candidate labels must be defined
structurally. The counts are only for the spin sectors of this one `H_E`
primitive family. They do not include the further nested outer holonomy
operations in `C_e(K)`, nor the complete W5 epsilon sum.


## Correction: charged-node multiplicity in the actual K-leg pipeline

The one-charged-node count above is valid for the single-source covariant
representation, but it is not the default output basis of
`peter_weyl_covariant_K_leg_gate.apply_HE_complete_key`: that function
projects onto `charged_nodes=(0,1)` by default. The custom K-composition
path can also request a single charged source, so the number of charged nodes
must be an explicit parameter, not an implicit convention.

For the initial `spins=(4,)*10` sector, two charged nodes give

`625^2 * 5^3 = 48,828,125`

ambient keys. This is the appropriate fixed-spin ambient dimension for the
two-charged-node `H_E` projection with nodes `(0,1)`. The earlier
`390,625` figure remains the correct one-charged-node dimension only.

For the 24 spin sectors of the representative `H_E) primitive family, the
two-charged-node ambient dimensions range from **5,760,000** to
**113,400,000**; their sum is **878,540,000**. These figures are structural
dimension counts, not matrix allocations or operator-reachable dimensions.
They make explicit why the full product basis cannot be materialized.

## Node-label propagation and volume selection

The generator now lifts every intermediate node to the complete
`(J2,M2,K12,K34)` basis. This is necessary because a holonomy hit changes
the local representation content at both endpoints; imposing Gauss singlets
at intermediate stages would be an unjustified restriction. Only after the
whole word is propagated does `project_all_charged_key` impose Gauss
`J2=M2=0, K12=K34` at nodes outside the requested charged-node set.

For the canonical volume operation, the current implementation constructs
a reduced block separately for each `J2` and reuses it at each `M2`.
Consequently the structural transition superset preserves `J2,M2` and
allows every admissible multiplicity pair `(K12,K34)) within that block.
This is a selection-rule superset: numerical zeros in the actual volume
matrix may make the true support smaller. The scalar-operator conservation
of total angular momentum and its magnetic component is the standard
Wigner-Eckart selection rule (see the cited angular-momentum references).

New functions include `charged_nodes_basis_size`,
`iter_charged_nodes_keys`, `volume_label_targets`,
`advance_hit_label_support`, `advance_volume_label_support`, and
`structural_sequence_support`. The latter keeps all nodes in complete
total-J sectors between hits and applies the final Gauss/charged projection
only at the end. It has an optional state-count guard that raises an error
rather than silently truncating the support.

## Adapter for the full oriented H_E primitive

The module now exposes `t_sequence_structural_support` and
`he_structural_support`. The latter mirrors the control-flow nesting in
`apply_HE_complete_key`: all `oriented_specs(source_v)`, both the forward
word and independently reversed adjoint word, and every actual
`T_sequences(v,a,b,c)` entry. It passes the *outer* `source_v` to volume
operations, rather than confusing it with the local `v` used to construct a
T-sequence. Numerical word coefficients and orientation signs are omitted
because this routine computes only an upper support bound.

A configurable `max_states` guard aborts with an explicit exception if the
support exceeds the requested memory budget; it never silently truncates.
The adapter is ready for bounded pilot runs on small spin sectors. It has not
yet been run on the full W5 seed because the multi-charged ambient spaces are
too large for indiscriminate enumeration.


## Composition through C(V), C(K), and the KKV triple

The structural layer now mirrors the nested operators used by
`bqg_v10_w5_lorentzian_adjoint_closure.py` and the covariant composition
modules:

[
C_e(V)=V-h_e Vh_e^{-1},qquad
K=[V,H_E],qquad
C_e(K)=K-h_eKh_e^{-1}.
]

It composes the exact representation-label upper bounds in the same
right-to-left order as the numerical operator:

[
T_{abc}= operatorname{Tr}_{aux}
ig[C_a(K)C_b(K)C_c(V)ig],
]

and constructs the adjoint independently in reversed order,

[
T_{abc}^{dagger}=
operatorname{Tr}_{aux}
ig[C_c(V)C_b(K)C_a(K)ig].
]

The intermediate source-sector filter after the middle leg is retained:
`J2 in (0,2)`, i.e. (J=0,1). All auxiliary-index choices are unioned;
magnetic-index zeros and numerical cancellations are deliberately ignored.
The node-level adapter traverses all 24 epsilon triples and unions forward
and reversed-adjoint supports. This is an upper support bound, not a
coefficient calculation.

Implemented functions:
`k_complete_structural_support`,
`C_volume_structural_support`, `C_K_structural_support`,
`triple_forward_structural_support`,
`triple_adjoint_structural_support`, and
`node_kkv_structural_support`. Each stage has a hard state-count guard that
raises instead of truncating. The full node-level KKV support has not yet been
enumerated: the multi-charge ambient spaces and nested operator branches can
exceed the current pilot memory budget.


## Exact rank-1/2 holonomy transition refinement

The initial hit propagator retained every admissible total-J sector at both
endpoints. This was safe but unnecessarily broad. Inspection of the actual
`PW.apply_hit_branch` and `hit_mats` implementation shows that each
fundamental holonomy insertion is a rank-1/2 tensor at each endpoint. The
Wigner-Eckart triangle rule therefore requires

[
|J-J'|\leq\tfrac12\leq J+J',
qquad
J'_2\in\{J_2-1,J_2+1\},
]

with negative labels excluded. The propagator now imposes this exact
representation-theoretic restriction independently at both endpoints.
All admissible (M'_2) values are still retained; no magnetic-index pruning
is attempted because the orientation and fixed-index convention belongs to
the coefficient engine. Thus the new rule reduces false candidate sectors
without assuming any numerical CG coefficient is nonzero beyond the
representation-theoretic selection rules.

A new test verifies that a fundamental hit from (J=0) reaches only
(J'=1/2), and from (J=1) reaches only (J'=1/2,3/2) in doubled-label
notation. This is an exact structural rule, not a numerical amplitude result.


## Magnetic-index pruning: deliberately not enabled

An attempted fixed-(M_2) transition rule was cross-checked against the
repository's actual `hit_mats`, `apply_hit_branch`, and
`project_branch_complete_charges` implementations for both edge
orientations. The first inferred sign convention did not reproduce the
reference projections, and a multi-edge path exposed intermediate-branch
charge leakage. That inference was therefore removed rather than weakening
the tests or retaining an unverified sign. The production structural support
continues to retain every admissible (M_2) while enforcing the exact
rank-1/2 total-(J) triangle rule. Tests now assert that actual fixed-index
CG projections are contained in the conservative structural support, not
that an unverified magnetic rule is exact.

## Factorized conservative envelope

The explicit joint-key pilot on the physical W5 (j=2,K=0) seed exceeds
5,000 states during the first encountered path
((0,1,2,0)): its preceding frontier has 1,840 states and the next expansion
reaches 5,001, so the guard aborts without truncation. This is a genuine
combinatorial bottleneck, not a numerical zero.

To avoid materializing the Cartesian product of all five node-label sets, the
module now also provides a factorized envelope. For each ten-link spin sector
it stores the marginal allowed label set at each of the five nodes. The
product of these local sets is an upper bound on the joint support; cross-node
correlations are not claimed. Functions:
`factorized_sequence_support`,
`factorized_t_sequence_support`, `factorized_contains_key`, and
`factorized_support_profile`. The factorized representation can report both
its compact storage size and the (possibly much larger) product-basis
dimension upper bound without enumerating that product.

Two tests compare this envelope with explicit one-hit and multi-edge path
supports and require every explicit key to be contained. A bounded pilot on
the physical W5 (K=0) seed is now part of CI. Its output must be interpreted
as a conservative envelope, not an exact joint reachable-state list.

## Factorized operator-level KKV propagator

A separate module, `scripts/bqg_w5_factorized_operator_support.py`, now composes the
operator stages directly on marginal supports, without constructing the
Cartesian product of node labels:

- `factorized_he`: all oriented H_E specs, actual repository T-sequences,
  and independently reversed adjoint words;
- `factorized_k`: both V-after-H_E and H_E-after-V branches of [V,H_E];
- `factorized_c_volume`: direct scalar-volume branch plus inverse-hit,
  volume, and closing-hit branch for C_e(V);
- `factorized_c_k`: direct K branch plus inverse-hit, K, and closing-hit
  branch for C_e(K);
- `factorized_triple`: forward C_a(K) C_b(K) C_c(V) and independently
  reversed adjoint C_c(V) C_b(K) C_a(K), retaining the frozen source-node
  J2 in {0,2} filter after the middle C(K) leg.

The representation is keyed by the ten-link spin sector and stores five
marginal local-label sets. Every union and expansion has a fail-closed
marginal-label guard. The product of marginal cardinalities is reported only
as an upper bound. The implementation does not preserve cross-node
correlations, enumerate exact joint keys, calculate coefficients, or infer
nonzero amplitudes.

The CI suite now includes inclusion checks against the exact joint-key
structural routines for K, C_e(K), the forward KKV triple, and the independently
reversed adjoint triple. These checks use a deliberately tiny mocked H_E
family (one empty word): they certify the composition order, projection
plumbing, and conservative marginal inclusion in that finite control, not the
full physical W5 word family or numerical operator.

A bounded physical-seed runner,
`scripts/bqg_w5_factorized_kkv_pilot.py`, exercises the ordered triple
(1,2,3) on the W5 K=0 seed at Jmax2=10. It reports forward and adjoint
marginal envelopes separately and treats a guard hit as a diagnostic result.
It explicitly marks amplitudes and joint-key enumeration as not performed.
